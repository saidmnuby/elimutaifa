This policy explains how the independent ElimuTaifa platform handles information when you use its education-information services.

Last updated: September 12, 2026

On this page Information we collect How we use it Independence and sources Security and limits Retention Advertising External links Contact

1. Information we collect

When you search for results, we process the examination index number and examination year you submit. The server may also receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details as part of normal web requests.

When you use the contribution form, we store the selected topic, the role or description you provide, your message, and any phone number or email address you choose to provide. A one-way hash derived from the request IP address is temporarily used to limit repeated submissions. Administrators may add an internal review note and status to the message.

For first-party operational analytics, public pages may send their path to ElimuTaifa. We aggregate page views and approximate unique visitors using a one-way server-side hash derived from the IP address and browser user-agent. We do not use this operational counter to create advertising profiles, and the browser Do Not Track signal is honoured by the page-view script.

System-health records may contain the requested portal path, examination level, external source host and a normalised source path, HTTP status, general error category, first and last occurrence times, and occurrence count. Candidate numbers and raw IP addresses are not intentionally stored in the traffic or system-event dashboards.

We do not ask for passwords, payment information, or unnecessary personal details to search examination results.

2. How we use information

  • To validate your request and retrieve the requested examination result.
  • To protect the service from abuse and excessive automated requests.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve system reliability.
  • To review and respond to questions, corrections, education announcements, suggestions, and problem reports submitted through the contribution form.
  • To understand aggregate traffic, identify missing portal URLs, detect unavailable or moved external result pages, and plan maintenance.

3. Independence and external information providers

ElimuTaifa is privately and independently operated.

It is not a government portal and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, controlled by, or operated on behalf of the Government of Tanzania, NECTA, NACTVET, TCU, TIE, or any other government institution, education authority, school or organisation.

What this independence means

  • ElimuTaifa does not represent an external institution and cannot speak, decide or act on its behalf.
  • ElimuTaifa cannot create, approve, cancel or change examination results, selections, admissions, registrations, awards or other official decisions.
  • References to institutional names, examination names or abbreviations identify the subject or original information source only. They do not indicate a partnership, endorsement or official status.
  • A link to an external website does not create an association between ElimuTaifa and the owner of that website.

External information and accuracy

ElimuTaifa may organise, explain, link to or help users locate publicly available education announcements, examination results, selection information, admission updates and learning opportunities produced by external providers. Ownership and responsibility for an external notice, result or decision remain with its original provider.

Although reasonable care may be taken when presenting information, external information can be changed, delayed, withdrawn, incomplete or incorrectly interpreted. ElimuTaifa does not guarantee that every item is complete, current or error-free.

User verification

Before acting on deadlines, payments, admission instructions, selection decisions, examination results or other important information, users should confirm the details directly with the institution responsible for publishing or administering them. Where the portal provides a source link, that link should be used for verification.

Corrections and concerns

If information appears incorrect, outdated, misleading or attributed to the wrong source, users can report it through the ElimuTaifa contact or contribution page. ElimuTaifa may correct or remove its presentation of that information, but only the original responsible institution can amend an official record or decision.

4. Security, caching, and request limits

Result pages may be temporarily cached on the server for up to five minutes to reduce repeated requests to external providers. Requests are limited per client to help protect the service and its providers. These controls are operational safeguards, not a guarantee of absolute security.

Contribution messages are stored in a database that is blocked from direct web access. Administrative access is protected by authentication, request-forgery controls, login throttling, time-limited sessions, and activity logging. No internet service can guarantee absolute protection against every security incident.

5. Data retention

Temporary result caches expire automatically after five minutes. Short-lived request-limit records are retained only for rate-limiting purposes and are continuously discarded as their one-minute window expires.

The IP-derived hash used for contribution rate limiting is cleared after its one-hour rate window during subsequent form processing. Contribution messages, optional contact details, administrative notes, and audit records are retained for moderation, support, accountability, and abuse investigation until they are no longer reasonably needed. Users may request review or removal of a submitted message using the contact details below, subject to information needed to identify the message.

Per-path unique-visitor hashes used for traffic calculations are kept for up to 90 days, recent anti-duplication records are removed after one day, and aggregate daily traffic totals are kept for up to 730 days. These cleanups occur periodically as traffic is processed. Resolved system events and administrative audit records may be retained longer where reasonably needed for reliability and accountability.

6. Advertising

ElimuTaifa may display advertisements or sponsored content in the future to support operation and development of the service. Advertising partners may use their own technologies and privacy policies. Advertising or sponsorship will not be presented as an official communication from NECTA, NACTVET, TCU, TIE or another external institution.

7. External links

The service may link to source institutions, education providers, social-media platforms and other external websites. Their content, availability, security and privacy practices are controlled by those providers. ElimuTaifa is not responsible for those external practices, and a link does not create an association or endorsement.

Some information articles may display an image hosted by an external provider. Loading that image can send the provider ordinary request details such as the user's IP address. Before a YouTube video is played, ElimuTaifa may load its cover image from YouTube so visitors can identify it; this image request may also share ordinary request information with YouTube or Google. The YouTube player itself is not loaded automatically and is created through the privacy-enhanced domain only after the user chooses to play the video. If another embedded video is started on the same page, the previously active player is removed to stop overlapping playback. YouTube and Google may process these requests under their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Terms of Service

ElimuTaifa is provided to help users locate publicly available education information and announcements. You agree to use the service lawfully, avoid abusive or automated requests, and verify important information with the original responsible institution. Access may be limited or changed to protect the service and external providers.
