This policy explains how ElimuTaifa uses information when you use our education services.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

On this page Information we collect How we use it Independence and sources Security and limits Retention Advertising External links Contact

1. Information we collect

When you search for results, we use the exam number and year you enter. Our server also receives technical details, such as your IP address and browser information, as part of the web request.

When you send feedback, we save your topic, role or description, message, and any phone number or email address you choose to share. We temporarily use a one-way code (hash) made from your IP address to limit repeated messages. Admins may add a private note and a review status.

Public pages may send their page address to ElimuTaifa so we can count visits. We estimate visitor numbers using a one-way code made from the IP address and browser details. We do not use these counts to build advertising profiles. The page-view script respects the browser’s Do Not Track setting.

Error records may include the page address, exam level, source website, a simplified source path, response status, error type, dates and number of repeats. We do not intentionally store candidate numbers or raw IP addresses in the traffic or error dashboards.

We do not ask for passwords, payment information, or unnecessary personal details to search examination results.

2. How we use information

  • To validate your request and retrieve the requested examination result.
  • To protect the service from abuse and excessive automated requests.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve system reliability.
  • To review and respond to questions, corrections, education announcements, suggestions, and problem reports submitted through the contribution form.
  • To understand aggregate traffic, identify missing portal URLs, detect unavailable or moved external result pages, and plan maintenance.

3. Independence and external information providers

ElimuTaifa is privately and independently operated.

It is not a government portal and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, controlled by, or operated on behalf of the Government of Tanzania, NECTA, NACTVET, TCU, TIE, or any other government institution, education authority, school or organisation.

What this independence means

  • ElimuTaifa does not represent an external institution and cannot speak, decide or act on its behalf.
  • ElimuTaifa cannot create, approve, cancel or change examination results, selections, admissions, registrations, awards or other official decisions.
  • References to institutional names, examination names or abbreviations identify the subject or original information source only. They do not indicate a partnership, endorsement or official status.
  • A link to an external website does not create an association between ElimuTaifa and the owner of that website.

External information and accuracy

ElimuTaifa helps people find and understand public education announcements, results, selections, admission updates and learning opportunities from other providers. The original provider remains responsible for its notice, result or decision.

We take care when showing information, but sources may change, delay or remove it. Information may also be incomplete or misunderstood. We cannot promise that every item is complete, current or free from errors.

User verification

Before relying on a deadline, payment request, admission instruction, selection or exam result, check with the institution responsible for it. Use the source link where one is provided.

Corrections and concerns

Report incorrect, old, misleading or wrongly credited information through our Contact or Feedback page. We may correct or remove what we show. Only the responsible institution can change an official record or decision.

4. Security, caching, and request limits

We may keep a temporary copy of a result page on the server for up to five minutes to reduce repeated requests to the source. We also limit requests from each client to protect both services. These steps reduce risks but cannot guarantee complete security.

Feedback messages are stored in a database that cannot be opened directly through the website. Admin access uses sign-in checks, protection against forged requests, limits on sign-in attempts, sessions that expire, and activity logs. No internet service can guarantee complete protection from security incidents.

5. Data retention

Temporary result copies expire after five minutes. Records used to limit requests are used only for that purpose and are removed as their one-minute window expires.

The IP-based code used to limit feedback messages is cleared after its one-hour window when later messages are processed. We keep messages, optional contact details, admin notes and activity records while reasonably needed for review, support and abuse investigations. You can request a review or removal using the contact details below. We may need details to identify your message.

Visitor codes for each page are kept for up to 90 days. Records used to prevent repeat counting are removed after one day. Daily traffic totals are kept for up to 730 days. Cleanup runs periodically as traffic is processed. Resolved error reports and admin activity records may be kept longer when reasonably needed for reliability and accountability.

Admins may show sponsor banners and cards on selected public pages. They are labelled as sponsored content, not exam results or government endorsements. We choose placements by page and schedule, not by tracking your behaviour. This MVP does not use third-party ad-network scripts or track sponsor clicks and impressions. Opening sponsor links or loading images from another website may share normal connection details with that provider. Its own privacy policy applies.

6. Advertising

We may add advertising or sponsored content to help pay for running and improving the service. Advertising partners may use their own tools and privacy policies. Ads will not be presented as official messages from NECTA, NACTVET, TCU, TIE or another institution.

7. External links

Links may take you to institutions, education providers, social media or other websites. Those providers control their content, availability, security and privacy practices. We are not responsible for those practices. A link does not mean a partnership or endorsement.

Some articles show images from other websites. Loading them may share request details, including your IP address, with the image provider. YouTube cover images may load before you press Play and may share those details with YouTube or Google. The video player loads through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain only after you press Play. Starting another video on the same page removes the previous player to stop both playing at once. YouTube and Google apply their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Terms of Service

ElimuTaifa helps you find public education information and announcements. Use the service lawfully. Do not send abusive or automated requests. Check important details with the responsible institution. We may limit or change access to protect the service and its sources.

9. Contact and updates

Questions about this policy can be sent to grf@elimutaifa or ceo@elimutaifa. You can also call 255 765 657 X77. We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change.
