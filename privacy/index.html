This policy explains how ElimuTaifa (ElimuTaifa) handles information when you use the examination-results service.

Last updated: September 4, 2026

On this page Information we collect How we use it Result providers Security and limits Retention Advertising External links Contact

1. Information we collect

When you search for results, we process the examination index number and examination year you submit. The server may also receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details as part of normal web requests.

We do not ask for passwords, payment information, or unnecessary personal details to search examination results.

2. How we use information

  • To validate your request and retrieve the requested examination result.
  • To protect the service from abuse and excessive automated requests.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve system reliability.

3. Official and external result providers

ElimuTaifa is an independent information-access service. It is not NECTA, is not an NECTA platform, and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by NECTA or any other examination authority or organisation. ElimuTaifa does not represent any organisation whose pages it links to.

ElimuTaifa may connect you to published information on official pages or trusted education-result archives. Please confirm important information on the relevant official page or with the responsible authority. ElimuTaifa does not change official results.

4. Security, caching, and request limits

Result pages may be temporarily cached on the server for up to five minutes to reduce repeated requests to external providers. Requests are limited per client to help protect the service and its providers. These controls are operational safeguards, not a guarantee of absolute security.

5. Data retention

Temporary result caches expire automatically after five minutes. Short-lived request-limit records are retained only for rate-limiting purposes and are continuously discarded as their one-minute window expires.

6. Advertising

ElimuTaifa may display advertisements or sponsored content in the future to support operation and development of the service. Advertising partners may use their own technologies and privacy policies. We will not describe an advertisement as an official NECTA communication.

7. External links

The service may link to NECTA, Edutech, social-media platforms, and other external websites. Their content, security, and privacy practices are managed by those providers. A link does not create an association or endorsement.

8. Terms of Service

ElimuTaifa is provided to help users locate publicly published examination information. You agree to use the service lawfully, avoid abusive or automated requests, and verify important information with the relevant official authority. Access may be limited or changed to protect the service and external providers.

9. Contact and updates

Questions about this policy can be sent to grf@elimutaifa or ceo@elimutaifa. You can also call 255 765 657 X77. We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change.
